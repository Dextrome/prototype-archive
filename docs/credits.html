FROM THE DEVELOPMENT NOTEBOOK

Media notes
& credits.

All imagery is selected from game development renders or clearly labeled staged concept scenes. Film editing, compression, crops and typography were prepared for this archive. No claim is made that a concept film demonstrates a finished game.

What the film labels mean

Gameplay trailer: captured from a running build, sometimes with bots or scripted ordinary player inputs. Forward cuts may compress time. Ant Delve uses a 3× simulation-speed demonstration.

Staged concept film: authored sequences communicate a proposed experience. The five factory films use deliberately scripted interactions, combat, outcomes and progression. Hellgrinder stages its machine and danger meter; Rot Garden Romance accelerates growth and skips dialogue timing while using real plant, reward and card rules. BlorpBrawl is a new procedural Godot concept scene, not Unreal gameplay.

Screenshot concept reel: restrained camera movement over genuine saved stills. AnthroWorld uses historical renderer captures; Card Scalper uses screenshots of its existing player build. They are not moving gameplay recordings.

Gurk’s extended feature trailer includes an original synthesized chamber score and edited sound effects from its CC0 game bank. All other films are silent. Screenshots and films may come from different development passes. Current game descriptions are grounded in project feature documents and source; an older capture can show an earlier interface or art treatment.

Gurk trailer production notes

The extended feature trailer uses the real Gurk renderer and existing core mechanics. Starting conditions, body and trap placement, the demonstration save, cameras and timing are staged for readable shots. Cleaning, rinsing, hauling, recycling, disposal, traps, pickups, gadgets and purchases use the game’s rules. It is a montage, not a continuous player session. The trash-radar interface is enlarged for legibility. Capture runs offscreen and does not record the desktop.

Audio: original synthesized chamber score for this trailer. Edited game foley is derived from CC0 recordings by rubberduck (OpenGameArt), Wareya (RPG Foley), and Kenney (50 RPG sound effects), as documented in Gurk’s audio provenance.

Gurk asset attribution

Gurk’s rendered media includes adapted game assets. Original model files are not distributed in this gallery.

  • Goblin (“Goblin animated by Motion capture”) and hero (“Animated Defender”) by Danimal, made for OpenDungeons. Blend Swap #77353 and #76788, CC BY 3.0. Converted and adapted materials in the game.
  • Dungeon Construction Kit by PSW Art, Blend Swap #92298, CC BY 3.0. Adapted in the game.
  • Modular Dungeon Set by coogowie, CGTrader model 2827549, adapted shelf dressing. Source and license. No standalone models are provided.
  • Handpainted Bookshelf by Shadowfeign / 7thSage, OpenGameArt, CC0; retained as an earlier runtime source/reference.
  • Environment overhaul: original procedural stonework, gothic bookcases, barrels and wood grain, plus an original generated stone surface.
